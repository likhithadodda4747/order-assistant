# Runbook > Out-of-stock item on an order

## Step 1: Confirm which items are out of stock
Check stock for every item on the order. Only items marked out of stock need action.

## Step 2: Offer the customer a choice
1. **Substitute** with a similar item of equal or lower price (no extra charge).
2. **Split shipment:** ship available items now, the rest when restocked (within 7 days).
3. **Remove** the out-of-stock item; the customer is not charged for it.

## Step 3: Update the order
Record the customer's choice on the order. If nothing is chosen within 48 hours, the out-of-stock item is removed automatically.
