# Runbook > Process a refund

1. Confirm the return was received and inspected.
2. Refunds go back to the **original payment method**.
3. Card refunds take **3–5 business days**; gift card refunds are instant.
4. Refunds above **$500** need manager approval (see "Refund approval limits").
